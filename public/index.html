Fast conversations leave people out

People with ADHD, autism or auditory processing differences lose the thread in fast, overlapping talk, and today's tools only help afterwards.

🧵

What you get, live

🔔 "They're asking you"

A calm banner shows the question and the two lines before it, the moment someone addresses you by name.

⚡ Catch me up

One tap gives a 10-second visual recap of the last few minutes. "What did I miss?" covers the time you looked away.

✅ A map you can trust

Decisions stay grey until a second model confirms them. Ideas nobody agreed to become open questions, not decisions.

🧭 You stay in control

Tap any item to see the exact quote behind it. Mark it wrong and the models won't add it back.
