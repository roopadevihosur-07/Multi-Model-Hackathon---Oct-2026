Use cases

Anywhere a fast conversation leaves someone behind.

Workplace meetings

An accommodation for a team member who needs the thread in planning and standup meetings.

Lectures and seminars

Disability services can offer live maps where note-taking support is hard to staff.

Interviews and panels

Hear when a question is aimed at you, and catch up on what the panel already covered.

Family and community meetings

Keep decisions and tasks visible when several people talk at once.

System architecture

One pipeline, five jobs, every claim traced to a line of speech.

Speech inDeepgram nova-3 streaming, with speaker labels
→
MapperGemini 2.5 Flash-Lite proposes topics and items, each citing transcript lines
→
VerifierDeepSeek, a different provider, checks each decision and task
→
Thread mapTopic patches, pending and confirmed decisions, audit trail
Alert checkFree name match first, then Gemini Flash-Lite, only when your name and a question appear
Catch-up and overviewClaude Sonnet writes three points, the meeting flow and "where it stands now"

If a provider is missing or fails, the router falls back to the next one. With no keys at all, local rules keep the demo running. Every call is priced in the cost ledger.

User workflow

From "everyone agreed" to a decision you confirmed.

  1. 1

    Everyone agrees

    Nothing is recorded until everyone in the room has agreed to be mapped. The consent is written to the audit trail.

  2. 2

    The map grows

    Topics appear as stitched patches in the order they came up. Existing patches never move under your eye.

  3. 3

    It's your turn

    When someone asks you something, one amber banner shows the question and the topic. It stays until you dismiss it.

  4. 4

    Check what was agreed

    Decisions wait as "Loose" until you tie them. Anything the checker isn't sure about can be kept or removed.

  5. 5

    Catch up calmly

    Three points from "Catch me up", or the Overview tab for how the meeting flowed and where it stands now.

Models

Each model chosen for its specific strength; integration unlocks capability.

Listen

Deepgram nova-3

Streaming speech-to-text with speaker labels, so the map knows who said what.

Keep up

Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite

Fast and inexpensive enough to update the map every few seconds, and to spot questions aimed at you.

Check

DeepSeek

A different provider from the mapper, so no model grades its own work.

Structure

Claude Sonnet

Best at turning a messy conversation into calm, short recaps and the meeting overview.

One large model could do every job, but at about five times the cost per hour, and without an independent check. Routing is what makes the checks affordable.

Judging criteria

How dottie meets each criterion.

Multi-model integration is essential

25%

Each model chosen for its specific strength; integration unlocks capability no single model has. Five jobs, two providers cross-checking, and fallbacks that keep it running.

Novel idea with clear differentiation

20%

Not a summary after the meeting. A live map, an alert when you're asked, and decisions that only count once a person ties them.

Production-ready and economically viable

15%

Automated end-to-end tests, an audit trail, and a cost ledger that shows routed cost against one large model, live in the app.

Compelling demo under pressure

20%

A scripted meeting runs through the real pipeline in about 45 seconds: an alert, a verification, a downgrade to "not sure", and a catch-up.

Solves a problem people care about

20%

Built for people who lose the thread in fast talk. Low sensory load is the design goal: one amber alarm, everything else calm.
