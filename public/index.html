The problem

In a fast conversation, a summary arrives after the decision has already been made.

Who gets left behind

People with ADHD, autism or auditory processing differences can lose the thread when several people talk at once and the topic jumps.

Why current tools do not help

Meeting tools summarise afterwards. The summary can miss who was asked a question, and it arrives after the moment to join in has passed.

What dottie does instead

It builds a map while people talk, alerts you when someone turns to you, and checks each decision against the words that were said. Target: an alert within 3 seconds.

Note-taking support is hard to staff in many lectures and meetings, so the thread is often lost before anyone can follow it.

User workflow

From "everyone agreed" to a decision you confirmed.

  1. 1

    Everyone agrees

    Nothing is recorded until everyone in the room has agreed to be mapped. The consent is written to the audit trail.

  2. 2

    The map grows

    Topics appear as stitched patches in the order they came up. Existing patches never move under your eye.

  3. 3

    It's your turn

    When someone asks you something, one amber banner shows the question and the topic. It stays until you dismiss it.

  4. 4

    Check what was agreed

    Decisions wait as "Loose" until you tie them. Anything the checker isn't sure about can be kept or removed.

  5. 5

    Catch up calmly

    Three points from "Catch me up", or the Overview tab for how the meeting flowed and where it stands now.
